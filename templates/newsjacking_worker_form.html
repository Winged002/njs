Recipe logic

The Worker uses these layers in order.

  1. News storyObserved from selected Sources
  2. Campaign contextSelected evidence + audience + strategic direction
  3. Supplemental evidenceOptional Worker-specific Collection items
  4. Product relevanceOnly products that naturally fit this specific story
  5. Editorial articleUseful news first, light contextual product bridge, proportionate CTA
Products are not blindly inserted.If the selected product pool has no defensible connection to a story, the Worker skips the match instead of manufacturing a promotional angle.
